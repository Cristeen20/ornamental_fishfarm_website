About the farm

A small family farm that takes water quality seriously.

Quality Ornamental Fish Farm started as a backyard hobby and grew into a full breeding unit. We keep our own brood stock, hatch our own fry and raise them on live and quality prepared feed — which is why our fish colour up faster and settle into a new tank without fuss.

Nothing leaves the farm before it has spent time in our quarantine tanks. We check for fin condition, appetite and parasites, so what you receive is exactly what you saw.

  • Own brood stock — no re-packed imports
  • Quarantined and treated before dispatch
  • Guidance on tank size, feeding and water changes
  • Hobbyists, aquarium shops and starter-kit buyers all welcome

Care tips

Five habits that keep fish alive

The advice we give every customer before they leave the farm.

  1. 01

    Float before you release

    Float the sealed bag in your tank for 20 minutes, then mix in tank water a little at a time over another 20. Never dump the transport water into the tank.

  2. 02

    Feed less than you think

    Only what they finish in two minutes, once or twice a day. Uneaten feed is the number one cause of cloudy water and sick fish.

  3. 03

    Change 25% weekly

    Swap a quarter of the water each week with dechlorinated water at the same temperature. Never do a full change — it wipes out your filter bacteria.

  4. 04

    Rinse the filter in tank water

    Wash sponge media in a bucket of old tank water, not under the tap. Chlorine kills the bacteria colony doing all the work.

  5. 05

    Quarantine new arrivals

    Keep new fish in a separate tank for a week before adding them to an established one. It saves the whole tank if something is brewing.
